AI-Native Developer
9-week Internship Program

From intern to AI-native
professional developer

Coding agents now write most of the syntax. What the industry still pays developers for is judgement: designing the database and the system, reading and questioning the code an agent produces, proving that the feature works before QA sees it, and shipping it to production. This program turns an intern who knows basic programming into that developer across 49 modules in 8 tracks over 9 weeks.

49 modules 8 tracks 9 weeks Scored 0 → 4, target = 3

What the agent does, and what you own

You direct the loop and sign off on the result. The agent does most of the typing in the middle. Every module in this program trains one of the boxes you own.

How to use this program

1
Learn the concept

Each module explains the ideas and diagrams you need to judge code. You are not asked to memorise syntax.

2
Practise with Claude Code

From week 2 you use Claude Code every day. Every module has a "Working with Claude Code" section.

3
Tick the evidence

The checklist at the end of each module lists what to show your mentor. The last item is always a small, real piece of work.

4
Review with your mentor

Once a week, score yourself 0 → 4 and have your mentor confirm the score against your evidence.

Scoring scale: 0 no exposure · 1 awareness · 2 works with support · 3 works independently (target) · 4 leads & coaches. Every intern takes the Core modules. You take the Elective modules that match your path.

Choose your path

Everyone shares the foundations, design, quality and deployment tracks. You make two choices with your mentor in week 1, based on the team you will join.

1 · Client: Web or Mobile
  • Web Frontend Engineering: FE1–FE3 (React, Next.js, styling) in week 3, then FE4–FE6 (forms, testing, performance & SEO) in weeks 7–9
  • Mobile App Engineering: MB1–MB2 (Flutter, state, navigation & forms) in week 3, then MB3–MB5 (data & device features, testing, release & performance) in weeks 7–9
  • Both paths first take AG4 AI Design Tools
2 · One backend stack
  • Everyone takes BE1 API Design and the SD track
  • Then pick one: BE2 Rails · BE3 NestJS · BE4 FastAPI · BE5 Gin
  • The concepts carry over between stacks. The stack module shows you where each concept lives in that framework.
