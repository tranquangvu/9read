The FDE is the engineer who embeds with a customer, finds the use case that matters, builds the AI system that solves it, and ships it into their production environment. This curriculum takes you from fullstack developer to master-level FDE across 44 skills in 4 tracks.

One engagement, end to end. Every skill in this curriculum maps to a stage of this loop.
